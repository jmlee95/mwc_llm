 Regarding the use of affiliates/coupons/ points

■ Use of affiliated companies and presentation of card

division Details

Take advantage of affiliate benefits

ㅇ You cannot use affiliate benefits simultaneously (overlapping) at the same time, but you can use each benefit on the same date.

 ex) VIP Choice at Baskin Robbins 
Present your membership card

ㅇ Since benefits are not provided at all affiliated stores, please use [Find a Store] on KT.COM.

    You must visit the store after checking if it is eligible for benefits.

ㅇ When using affiliate benefits, you must present your KT membership card regardless of whether points are deducted.

 - The membership card must be used by the person who purchased it, and identification may be requested for verification purposes.

 

■ Coupon confirmation and use unavailable

division Details
Coupon Verification Path

ㅇ If you have been issued a coupon to use KT membership benefits, you can check it through the path below.

 - KT.COM: My > Membership/Benefits > My Membership > View at a glance > Membership Coupon Box

 - KT Membership App: [Coupon] at the top of the main page or [More] at the bottom right of the main page > Coupon Box or My > [Coupon Box] at the bottom

 - MyKT App: Menu > My > Membership/Benefits > My Coupons > Membership Coupon Box or Benefits > Barcode Top Right [Coupon]
Coupons cannot be used

ㅇ When you click the [Complete Coupon Verification] button, you can use the coupon. When you click the [Complete Use] button, the coupon is canceled and cannot be used.

 ※ ☞ [Complete coupon verification], [Complete use] Details and examples

 

■ Point shortage and line stop

division Details
Points shortage If you do not have enough points, your application will be rejected by all affiliates that deduct points.
Line stop

ㅇ Points cannot be used when all products subscribed to by KT are suspended.

 - Regardless of the reason for suspension, points can be used as long as at least one line is in use.
Offline ㅇ The error message 'Points usage restricted due to line stop' is displayed on *1) POS* and is processed as unapproved.
Online ㅇ 'All of your KT lines have been suspended, so point use is restricted.

     To use membership, you must cancel the suspended line.' Error message pops up
