How to apply for a merger

division Details
Computer screen

 
Processing order ① After checking customer information, select [New] as the order type.
② Check wireless product information in [Individual Service Agreement] at the bottom and click the [+] button to add.
③ Select the combination type (Our Family Wireless Discount) and contract period (No contract).
④ Select [Our Family Wireless Discount] as the combination type for discount information.
⑤ If there are no errors in the pre-check, complete the order by registering the form and saving it.

 

 

How to check binding

division Details
Computer screen

 
Verification order ① Click [Our Family Wireless Discount] at the bottom
② Our Family Wireless Discount Management window pops up
③ In the Our Family Wireless Discount item [Check] The lines that are checked are the lines that receive the discount.

 

 

 How to convert  a total amount

division Details
Computer screen

 
Processing order ① After checking customer information, select [Product Conversion] as the order type.
② Check the wired product information in [Individual Service Contract] at the bottom and click the Add [+] button.
③ Select the combination type (home) and contract period (no/1 year/2 years/3 years).
④ Select [Total combination discount] as the combination type of discount information.
⑤ Select whether to apply contribution discount by line or designated line discount.
⑥ If there is no error in the pre-check, complete by registering the form and saving the order.
※ Changing the discount option (total combination discount) and adding a line cannot be processed at the same time because the order types are different.
   Convert to total combination and then add a line, or complete adding a line and then change to total combination.
