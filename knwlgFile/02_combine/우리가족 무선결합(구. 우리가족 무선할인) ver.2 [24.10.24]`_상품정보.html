■ Release date: July 1, 2014 (regular product from 2015.01.01)

ㅇ Maximum number of combined lines: 5 lines (up to 2 lines under the same name allowed)

Discount conditions

ㅇ Discounts are provided only when combined by the end of the following month after new opening/excellent service change/renewal of contract

 - Newly opened lines (including used phones/phones purchased overseas) are provided with combined discounts without signing up for a contract (applied on 2023.07.03)

 - When using LTE/5G Direct or Yogo rate plans, discounts are available if you sign up for a new plan/change to a good plan by the end of the following month.

ㅇ 12-month/24-month contracts both offer a 24-month discount 

ㅇ Discount is applied only when using the sponsored rate plan or LTE/5G Direct/Yogo rate plan, and the discount amount is applied differently depending on the monthly fee
 - It does not have to be the rate plan of the sponsor currently in use. Ex) Discount is available when an LTE sponsored subscriber uses the i-Value rate plan.
Note

ㅇ Combination and discounts are provided starting from 2 lines, and the combination is canceled when only 1 line remains.

ㅇ A representative line must be designated when combining for the first time (however, the discount will continue to be provided even if the representative line is deleted)
ㅇ Combination is possible for customers who have passed the end of the following month after new opening/excellent contract change/renewal, but no discount will be provided (applied as of 2015.01.26)
    However, only the line is recognized without discount, regardless of whether or not the person is a sponsor/contractor

■ Discount applied

division Details
How to apply discount ㅇ Discount provided by calculating the number of days worked
ㅇ Pre-combination method provided (For details, refer to the ☞ [For wireless consultation] Common guidelines for combination discount document)
 - If verification and qualification processing are completed within the same month after combination application, discount is applied from the date of combination application
Discount application period

ㅇ Discount provided for 24 months (730 days) after combination (applied on July 15, 2017)
ㅇ Discount ends on the 2nd anniversary of the start date of combination discount, and discount is applied until the day before the end date
 . Ex) Combination discount starts on June 1, 2022 → Discount is applied until May 30, 2024 (Discount ends on May 31, 2024, and discount is applied until the day before the end date)

 - The discount ends, but the combination remains, and you can get a discount when signing up for a contract again.

ㅇ In case of combination after new/excellent contract/renewal: Provided for 24 months (730 days) from the time of combination application (pre-combination applied)
ㅇ In case of new/excellent contract/renewal after combination: Provided for 24 months (730 days) from the time of new/excellent contract/renewal

ㅇ Our family wireless combination provides a discount for 24 months from the application date if you are eligible in the combination
    application month. However, if you are not eligible in the application month, a discount is provided for 24 months (730 days) from the discount start date.
 Ex) If you apply for combination on 2017.09.20 and are eligible for combination on 2017.11.15, the discount will be applied from 2017.11.01 and provided for 24 months from 2017.11.01.
Discount application conditions

ㅇ Maintain the combined group only when there are 2 or more lines (cancel the combination if only 1 line remains)
ㅇ Combined lines 1 to 5 are simply ranked, and existing customer lines do not receive discounts
ㅇ Discounts are provided if the line is tied to the combination regardless of the end point of discounts for other lines

ㅇ No combination contract or penalty
ㅇ When using a discounted rate plan, a discount is provided according to the monthly fee
ㅇ If a discounted line is deleted and recombined after the end of the following month after a new/changed/renewed contract, no discount is provided
