  Regarding subscription

 

Q1. Can foreigners also sign up?
A1. Since December 2015, the computer system has been improved, so foreigners can combine their family members with the same standards as Koreans.
     Previously, due to computer limitations, foreigners could only combine up to two lines with the same name.
 
Q2. If the last day of the following month after opening falls on a weekend, is there an exception where you can sign up by the following Monday? 
A2. No. You must combine by the preceding Friday to receive the discount.

 

Q3. In the case of a simple discount, can I apply while receiving a sponsor discount? In this case, can I receive a combined discount?
A3. No. If there is an overlap between sponsor and simple discount, the initial sponsor is applied.
     Discounts are provided to customers who have re-signed (simple discount) after their sponsorship has ended.

 

Q4. There is a subscription period limit of the end of next month.

     After the end of next month, if our family wireless combination customer changes the combination line, can the combination be deleted and then added again?
A4. Combination is possible, but since it will be combined with the existing line, the existing discount will not be provided and only the non-discounted line will be recognized.

 

 

Discount related

 

Q1. My family's existing line that was tied to a wireless connection has been changed to a good line, so I registered a contract. Will the discount be automatically applied from next month?
A1. Yes, the discount is automatically applied and automatically reflected in the computer.

     In addition, automatic discounts are provided to customers who renew their contract with a simple discount or to customers who canceled their excellent contract and then changed to a new excellent contract.

 

Q2. Is there a mandatory renewal period for customers who renew their contracts for Our Family Wireless Plan to receive discounts?
A2. No. You can receive discounts by combining simple discounts or rate discounts (optional contracts) for 12/24 months.

Q3. What happens if I change the name while using our family wireless combination?
A3. After the computer integration, the benefits are inherited when
     the name is changed. However, if you delete only the line to be changed from the combined lines among multiple lines and then change the name and add it, it cannot be restored, so you
     must cancel the entire combination, change the name, and then cancel the cancellation through [A] Cancel combination cancellation.
    Ex) If you need to change the name of C to D among A+B+C combinations, you can restore the combination to A+B+D by changing the name of C to D after canceling the combination,
         but if you cancel the combination cancellation, the combined discount benefits will not be restored for lines whose sponsorship has ended.

 

Q4. After opening, if I do not use our family wireless connection, can I receive a discount when I change the name and apply for connection?

A4. If the transferee applies for our family wireless combination within the end of the following month after the transferor's new/excellent mobile phone contract/renewal, the combination benefit can be provided.

     However, if you apply for a new wireless connection with our family or add a line after the end of the following month after the transferor's new/excellent contract/renewal contract, the benefit cannot be applied.

 

Q5. If I use 3 combined lines and the 24-month discount for each line ends, will the combination be automatically canceled?
A5. Even if the 24-month discount for all combined family lines ends, the combination will not be automatically canceled and will remain in effect.

 

Q6. I was receiving a discount after opening a new account and then changed to a superior plan. Will the discount be applied again?
A6. You can receive a new discount for 24 months from the date of the superior plan change.

 

 

Related to binding features

 

Q1. What is a representative line?
A1. It is a line representing a family relationship. It must be designated when first combined, and even if the representative line is deleted, the combination will continue to be provided.

 

Q2. Is there a separate line that serves as a standard for proving family relationships?
A2. There is none. When first combined, if a family relationship is proven based on any one line, it is possible.

 

Q3. If the representative line is deleted after combining the first three lines, which of the remaining two lines can be used as a basis for proving the family relationship to add a line?
A3. Each line is the basis.

     For example, if you can prove a family relationship with B or C after deleting representative line A among A, B, and C, you can apply for a combination.

 

Q4. The existing wired/wireless combination confirms family relationships based on the Internet, but Our Family Wireless Combination confirms family relationships based on each combined mobile line.
     Then, how should I verify family relationship documents when switching from Our Family Wireless Combination to Total Combination?
A4. When switching to Total Combination, if the Internet owner is a member of the existing Our Family Wireless Combination, no family relationship verification documents are required.
     However, if the Internet owner is not a member of the existing Our Family Wireless Combination, family relationship verification documents must be submitted to verify the entire mobile line and 

     Proof of family relationship is required.

     If the Internet subscriber does not have a family relationship with the entire mobile line, transfer is not possible.
